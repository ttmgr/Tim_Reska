Doctoral thesis

Nanopore Environmental Metagenomics for One Health: A Tiered Framework for Detection and Attribution

I build sequencing pipelines that find pathogens and antibiotic resistance genes in air, water and food. I also tested whether 28 AI models could build the same pipelines. None built the harder one fully correctly, and many of their answers looked correct but were wrong.

Since July 2026 I work in medical underwriting. I judge health risk from medical evidence, calibrate risk surcharges on large, noisy data, and build disease progression models.

Genomic surveillance for One Health

Nanopore sequencing workflows that detect pathogens, resistance genes and their hosts in air, water, food and clinical samples. I was a founding member of an international research group that built its lab and computing setup from scratch across 7 sites. Seven publications came out of it, including one in Nature Communications.

As first author

  • 2024

    Air monitoring by nanopore sequencing

    Reska T, Pozdniakova S, Urban L. ISME Communications

    Nanopore sequencing can profile the microbes in air down to species level, even from the small amounts of fragmented DNA an air sampler collects. First data from Barcelona point to stable air microbiomes specific to each location.

    Pipeline code

  • 2026

    Real-time genomic pathogen, resistance, and host range characterization from passive water sampling

    Perlas A*, Reska T*, et al. Applied and Environmental Microbiology. *Shared first authors. 12 wetlands in 3 countries.

    Passive samplers were left in the water, and what they caught was sequenced with nanopore. Wetlands shaped by people carried more pathogens and resistance genes than natural ones, and avian influenza turned up at a third of the sites.

    Pipeline code

  • 2026

    A metagenomic framework for rapid Listeria monocytogenes surveillance

    Muchaamba F, Reska T, Biggel M, et al. bioRxiv preprint

    In a test community of known strains, standard culture testing recovered only some Listeria strains and missed mixed contamination. Sequencing the sample directly found all of them.

Against Plausibility

A benchmark of 28 AI models, most of them from the OpenAI, Claude and Gemini families. Each model was asked to build two of my published pipelines step by step, so the correct answer for every step was known in advance. One pipeline can be solved and one cannot. I scored all 476 steps on tool choice, parameters, output compatibility, scientific validity and whether the code runs. The models were tested between February and April 2026.

PipelineStepsModels with full marks on every step
Air79 of 28
Wetland100 of 28

In 3 of the 10 wetland steps, no model gave a fully correct answer. GPT-5, Opus 4.5 and Gemini 3 Pro were the first models in their families to complete the air pipeline.
